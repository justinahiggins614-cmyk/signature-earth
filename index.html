Our mission

Signature Earth is Manon's own window on the planet — built free for everyone. No account, no tracking, no ads. Spin a living 3D globe, search 69,763 real places from an open world gazetteer, measure the distance between any two points, save your favorite spots, and ride guided tours across the continents. Every coordinate is a real gazetteer record — nothing invented.

How to use it (3 steps)

  1. Spin & zoom — drag the globe with your finger or mouse, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  2. Search any place — type a city or town, tap it, and the globe flies you there with its real facts.
  3. Measure, tour, save — tap 📏 to measure between two taps, 🧭 for guided fly-through tours, ⭐ to keep your places.

What lives where

🏠 Main (this page) — the front door. 🌍 Globe — the interactive planet with search, measurer, tours, and saved places. 🗂 Places A–Z — the full gazetteer archive, last tab, marching toward one million real places.
