Welcome to Signature Earth

Your own planet explorer — spin the globe, dive to house level on the map, and let Terra the AI drive. Here’s how to use it:

  1. Globe or Map. The globe spins the whole planet — switch to Map mode to zoom all the way down to houses, like the big map sites.
  2. Search anything. Type a place or a street address — the map flies there and drops a pin.
  3. Ask Terra. The AI pal controls the map for you: “take me to my childhood house”, “measure from here to there”, “full scan this area”.
  4. Scans and Finds. Run treasure-hunter, scientist, or prospector scans over any view — save discoveries to the Finds tab.

How to use this site

🌎 Globe view

What: the 3D planet. Does: Drag to spin, pinch to zoom — compass and tilt controls, tap for coordinates, 3D terrain. How: Open the Globe tab and drag.

🗺 Map mode

What: the 2D map. Does: Zooms from the whole planet down to house level on real aerial imagery. Where deep imagery isn’t available yet, the map says so honestly. How: Tap Map, then pinch or use + / −.

🧬 Terra AI

What: the AI pal. Does: Conversational and drives the map: go-to, zoom, measure, layers, scans — and remembers your conversation. How: Type plain words, e.g. “zoom to house level” or “turn on borders”.

🔍 AI scans

What: the scan modes. Does: Treasure-hunter finds pattern anomalies, Scientist explains formations, Prospector flags surface indicators — always labeled as clues, never confirmed deposits. How: Ask Terra for a scan, or run “full scan” for all three at once.

📍 Finds tab

What: the discoveries catalog. Does: Interesting, unexplained, and valuable finds with map links — plus your own saved discoveries. How: Open Finds, tap any find to fly to it.

📏 Measure

What: the measure tool. Does: Distance with 2 points, area with 3 or more — works in globe and map modes. How: Tap Measure, then tap the map.

📋 Places, Tours, Saved

What: the gazetteer tabs. Does: Places A–Z browses 69,000+ places, Tours are guided flights, Saved keeps your pins. How: The tabs on the main page.
